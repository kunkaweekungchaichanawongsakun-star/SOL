She woke up when someone pulled the heavy curtains open. Still half asleep, she saw a man's dark shape against the window and, thinking it was Win, apologised to him with a faint, guilty smile for missing her new job. The moment Aliz sees it is Jack, the warmth leaves her face. She pulls herself back on the pillow to keep her distance and asks how he got here. Jack likes the question, because he enjoys telling this story, and he pulls a chair up to the bed to sit down.
