SCENE
Win's phone lights up in the dark with her call. He looks up at her lit window, a smile at the corner of his mouth, and answers quietly. Hearing his voice, Aliz comes alive: she tells him she is out of hospital and asks him, sweetly, to pick her up tomorrow. He says yes at once, and her smile grows. Excited, she tells him W-Group has asked her back for another interview. Win is truly glad for her, then looks down and smiles to himself, because he knows why W-Group called her.
