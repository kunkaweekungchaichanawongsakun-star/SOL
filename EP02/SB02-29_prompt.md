Win is across the street from her dorm, sitting sideways on the seat of his motorcycle on the pavement to the left of the closed kiosk, under a tree and a street lamp. She has a small plaster on the inside of her left forearm where the hospital drip was.

SCENE
Happy and puzzled, Aliz wonders aloud why this company is so kind to her; the audience knows the answer. Win apologises for leaving her alone at the hospital that day. She tells him that taking her there was already more than enough. Then Win, carefully and with a little jealousy he keeps down, tells her he went back and saw a man sitting with her, so he stayed away, and he waits for her answer.
