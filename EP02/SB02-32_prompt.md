BEFORE THIS VIDEO
All of this has already happened before the first frame. This is the ninth and last video of this scene. Aliz has just been discharged from hospital and is in her dorm room at night, in the pink knit top and white jeans she wore to her job interview, her hair tied up in a loose bun. She has just finished a sweet phone call with Win, the man she calls "ลุง", and was still smiling at herself in the mirror when her phone lit up on the bed with a voice message from Jack. Jack paid her hospital bill. One sneaker she pushed off earlier lies on the rug.

SCENE
Aliz sits back down on the edge of her bed and plays Jack's voice message, a faint smile still left on her face. Jack tells her the hospital bill has come: over three hundred thousand baht. Instead of paying him back, she only has to come and do the one job he asked her for, and they will be even. As she listens, the smile leaves her face and the weight of it settles on her. The night that should have been her best ends with a debt of three hundred thousand.
