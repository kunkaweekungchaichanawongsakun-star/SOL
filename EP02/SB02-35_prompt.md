SCENE
Aliz answers his worry with a joke: a hospital that expensive could hardly fail to cure her, but next time he can take her somewhere cheaper. Win hears the word money and worries she had to pay the bill herself, so he asks her straight out. She shakes her head: someone paid for her, and she would rather not talk about it. Win can hear in her voice that it is the man she wanted to stay away from, and that she still feels the same; he is quietly glad, and keeps the smile to himself. She closes the subject quickly and her bright mood comes back, and Win lets it go for her: he hands her her white helmet, because it is time to go.
