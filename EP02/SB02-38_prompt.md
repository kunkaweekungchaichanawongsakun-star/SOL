BEFORE THIS VIDEO
All of this has already happened before the first frame. This is the first video of a new scene. This morning Win picked Aliz up at her dorm on his motorcycle. She told him the plan had changed: instead of her interview at W-Group, his own company, which she is unaware of, she has to do a job first for Jack, the man who paid her hospital bill, to repay him, so that she is free of every debt. Win accepted it, closed his visor and rode her across the city to Jack's office building in the business district.

SCENE
The motorcycle arrives in front of a tall glass office building and stops at the kerb. Aliz gets off, takes off her white helmet and tidies her hair, because she is about to go to work, and hands the helmet back to Win. Win stays on the motorcycle with his visor down, facing the road: this is her business, and he keeps himself out of it. Jack has been waiting for her inside, and the moment she arrives he comes out through the revolving door, smiling, and walks straight to her across the pavement; she is all he is looking at. Aliz turns and sees him, and her smile becomes a polite one, because he is the man she wanted to stay away from, and today she owes him.
