BEFORE THIS VIDEO
All of this has already happened before the first frame. This is the first video of a new scene. Win, whom Aliz knows only as "ลุง", the man who takes her on his motorcycle every morning, is really Win Yu, "คุณวิญญู", the chairman of W-Group, and that is still his secret. A few days ago Aliz collapsed with a fever and was taken into a private hospital, and Win stayed by her bed until work called him away. While he was gone, Jack, a younger man who chases her, came to visit her, and Aliz now believes Jack paid her hospital bill. Win asked Nuch, his secretary, to find out what happened at the hospital. Now it is the afternoon, and Win is alone in his office at the top of the W-Group tower.

SCENE
Nuch comes into the chairman's office with the answer. Jack told the hospital staff he was the patient's husband so that they would let him in, and the whole bill was taken from Win's own account. Win finds the word "husband" absurd and laughs at it, because he sees exactly what kind of man Jack is. Then he remembers Aliz telling him that what she hates most is people who lie to your face, and he smiles, the smile he keeps only for her. The smile fades, because he realises that he is lying to her face too, every single morning, in the green jacket that hangs by his door.
