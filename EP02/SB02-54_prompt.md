Aoy stands on the stage in the short qipao made for the launch. Tin, the owner of the project, sits in the middle chair of the VIP row with an iPad. Juk ran all the way down to the stage to tell Aoy that the big man wants her to take off her black tights, and she refused with an excuse: her legs are blotchy and would spoil the launch. Juk has just run all the way back up and is bent beside Tin's chair, telling him her answer behind the iPad. Aoy calls Juk "พี่แจ็ค", Juk calls Tin "เฮีย", and Tin calls himself "ไอ".

SCENE
Tin is losing patience: he wants to see her legs and judge for himself. Juk says yes again, but the long steps down to the stage are already wearing him out, and this second run is much slower. Out of breath, he forces a sweet smile and plays the kind big brother: try taking them off, and if it really looks bad he will explain why she needs them. His mind is on his boss. Aoy, flustered, stalls with a joke: can it wait until tomorrow, she needs to shave her shins tonight. Juk is fed up and drags himself all the way back up. This time he and Tin talk for a long time behind the iPad, and Aoy, watching them from the stage, starts to understand that the next round will be different.
